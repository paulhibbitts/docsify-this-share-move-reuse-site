# Why It Matters

<small>Part 1</small>

> [!TIP]
> **Learning Objectives**
>
> - **Share:** see one set of pages work on any device, in an LMS and in print
> - **Move:** keep a guide open to adapt or self-host, with plain Markdown
> - **Reuse:** get the Markdown behind any page, with no source files needed

**Open for reuse:** an open licence gives you the [5Rs](https://opencontent.org/definition/) – retain, reuse, revise, remix and redistribute – but they all depend on getting at the pages themselves. Share, Move, Reuse is what makes them practical.

> [!NOTE]
> **The DIY answer: plain Markdown**
>
> A simple text format for web pages – `**bold**`, `## Heading`, `- list item`. It's supported almost everywhere, from GitHub and note-taking apps to Google Docs. Keep every page on a free site you control.

**Not ready to openly license your work?** Plain Markdown still keeps it yours – it outlasts any one platform, and moves, backs up and adapts easily for years to come.

Best of all for reuse, you (and, with an open licence, anyone) can get the Markdown behind any page – no source files needed – with its callouts, math and diagrams intact, ready for [Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/) (which shows Markdown as web pages) or GitHub.

**See the proof:** [a Pressbooks book](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/universaldesign2/) and [the same book in Open Publishing Space](https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/variants/grav-open-publishing-quark2-pressbooks-udl/) or [on Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/?basePath=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-udl-site/main&homepage=home.md&sidebar=true&loadSidebar=_sidebar.md&loadNavbar=_navbar.md&pagination=true&searchbox=true&collapsibleSidebarGroups=true&collapseSidebarGroups=true&dark-mode=auto&name=Universal%20Design%20for%20Learning%20%28UDL%29&edit-link=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-publishing-pressbooks-udl-site/main/home.md&edit-link-text=This%20page%20as%20Markdown&edit-link-emoji=:page_facing_up:), converted with no revisions – more in [From Pressbooks](open-publishing-space/from-pressbooks.md).

**One guide, many uses:** read it at your own pace, present it like slides (← and → move between pages), embed it in an LMS, print it or get its Markdown – all from the same pages. Write once, use it many ways.
